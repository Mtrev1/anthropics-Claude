    Briefing

    Frequently asked.

    Are these products for human use?

    No. All products are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory and research use. They are not drugs, supplements or cosmetics, and are not for human or veterinary consumption.

    What ships with each lot?

    A certificate of analysis: the HPLC purity trace, mass-spectrometry identity confirmation against the theoretical mass, an LAL endotoxin result, the lot number and analysis date.

    How are peptides packaged and stored?

    Lyophilized, sealed under inert argon, and shipped in insulated, temperature-monitored packaging. Store lyophilized vials frozen and protected from light.

    Can I verify a lot after it arrives?

    Yes. Every vial carries a lot number that resolves to its full analytical report for the life of the batch.

    Who can order?

    Orders are intended for qualified researchers, institutions and laboratories. By purchasing you confirm the products will be used solely for legitimate research in compliance with applicable law.
