§04 — Notes

Footnotes & frequently asked.

1 Are these for human use?

No. All products are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory and research use. They are not drugs, supplements or cosmetics, and are not for human or veterinary consumption.

2 Are the displayed sequences real?

The one-letter primary structures shown are the published sequences for each peptide, presented for identification. Certain research peptides also contain modified or non-standard residues that a single-letter code cannot fully represent.

3 What is on the certificate of analysis?

Each lot COA includes the HPLC purity trace, mass-spectrometry identity confirmation, an LAL endotoxin result, the lot number and the analysis date.

4 How are peptides shipped and stored?

Lyophilized, sealed under inert gas, and shipped with insulated, temperature-monitored packaging. Store lyophilized vials frozen and protected from light.
