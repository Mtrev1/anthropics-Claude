Before you ask

Frequently asked

Are these products for human use?

No. All products are supplied strictly for laboratory and in-vitro research use. They are not drugs, supplements, or cosmetics, and are not for human or veterinary consumption, ingestion, or any therapeutic use.

What does the certificate of analysis include?

Every batch COA includes the HPLC purity trace with quantified area-under-curve, mass-spectrometry identity confirmation against theoretical molecular weight, an LAL endotoxin screen, the lot number, and the analysis date.

How are the peptides shipped and stored?

Peptides are lyophilized and sealed under inert gas, then shipped in insulated packaging with temperature indicators. On receipt, store lyophilized vials frozen and protected from light per the guidance included with your order.

Can I verify a batch after it arrives?

Yes. Each vial carries a scannable lot identifier that resolves to its full analytical report, so any result can be traced back to its certificate for the life of the lot.

Who can order?

Orders are intended for qualified researchers, institutions, and laboratories. By purchasing you confirm the products will be used solely for legitimate research purposes in compliance with all applicable laws in your jurisdiction.
